# flymusicman

Projects for [Fly Music Man](https://www.youtube.com/@FlyMusicMan).

## [upload-portal](upload-portal/)

A footage drop page: fans send videos from their phone straight into a Google
Drive folder, agree to a footage release, and can leave a name for credits and
an email for future prize giveaways. Runs on Google Apps Script.
Start with [upload-portal/SETUP.md](upload-portal/SETUP.md).
